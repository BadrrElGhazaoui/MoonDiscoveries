<p><b>Kings Amidst Chaos</b></p>
<p>This picture shows the late King Hassan 2 in his 60's birthday with his son at the time Prince Mohammed 6, it shows how Hassan 2 is thinking, always. While the prince's face is bright like a fresh breath for the country. His father had survived more assasinations then he can remember, which have made his personality hard, very hard actually that he inhereted a country with many internal threats from Islamist organizations to the very tough military generals like oufkir and dlimi, and outside threats with spain taking the sahara and algeria threatening the country with an invasion. very far allies in the middle east and upper europe, the throne had to be preserved. In an interview with one of the most promiments figures in hasssan 2's regime : Driss Basri , after he was relieved from his missions he said : "Yes, we did it the hard way, but we made a country.
