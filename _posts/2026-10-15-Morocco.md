<p><b>Kings Amidst Chaos</b></p>
<p>This picture shows the late King Hassan 2 in his 60's birthday with his son at the time Prince Mohammed 6, it shows how Hassan 2 is thinking, always. While the prince's face is bright like a fresh breath for the country. His father had survived more assassinations than he can remember, which have made his personality hard, very hard actually that he inherited a country with many internal threats from Islamist organizations to the very tough military generals like Oufkir and Dlimi, and outside threats with Spain taking the Sahara and Algeria threatening the country with an invasion. very far allies in the Middle East and upper Europe, the throne had to be preserved. In an interview with one of the most prominent figures in Hassan 2's regime: Driss Basri, after he was relieved from his missions he said: "Yes, we did it the hard way, but we made a country. It had to be done with a sword".</p>

<p><b>The rise of a new King</b></p>
<p>
  Mohammed the 6, didn't just inherit a normal country, he inherited the 4th oldest kingdom in the planet, thousands of years of history and many more to come. Now that the French occupation has left the country, the new king has one mission : to build a country from ash. Hassan 2 had many enemies that left their followers wanting to take revenge, Benbarka and his left buddies were one, Abdesalam Yassine and his group were second, 3rd were the people, after what they saw at Tazmamart and what Driss Basri did to them, they were afraid. For external conflicts, the neighbor Algeria was watching closely as Abdelaziz Bouteflika was supporting the Polisario Front with military equipment, Spain, France and the Arab world were all watching.
</p>

<p>
  20 February 2011, it was Morocco's turn, everyone went out, it was chaos. Here, a decision was taken that I deem one of the smartest moves I have ever seen. November 2011, Abdelilah Benkirane was appointed prime minister of Morocco. Why is this a smart move? Benkirane was the closest political personality to the people, when you hear him speaking, he is simple and straightforward, doesn't speak a lot of classic Arabic and French, direct Darija. Plus Benkirane was from the party that was known to be inspired from Islamic instructions. And here was the checkmate, everything went down. And that year has seen 2011 Constitution that we still use to this day.
